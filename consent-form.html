What Friends does

Friends lets your child share photos of their art with families you connect with, and leave likes and kind comments. Only families you approve can see anything your child shares.

What Friends keeps

  • A first name or nickname you choose for your child, and a color
  • Photos of art your child shares, and the names they give them
  • Comments and likes your child leaves on friends’ art
  • Your name and email, so you can sign in and manage your family

It doesn’t ask for last names, addresses, schools, birthdays, or location. It has no ads and no tracking, and it doesn’t sell or share information with anyone else. A comment filter blocks unkind words and personal details like phone numbers and links.

Your choices

At any time, in the app’s grown-up area, you can see and delete anything your child shared, stop sharing with any family, or delete your family from Friends completely. Deleting your family also deletes this form.
