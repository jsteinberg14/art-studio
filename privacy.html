Privacy notice

My Art Studio is a small, invite-only app for families who know each other. It is run by the app owner, who you can reach at .

Art that isn’t shared

Pictures saved in the Museum stay on the device. They aren’t uploaded unless your child shares one with Friends.

What Friends collects

  • From parents: your name, your email address (to send sign-in codes), and the signed permission form.
  • About each child, entered by a parent: a first name or nickname and a color.
  • From children, after a parent gives permission: photos of art they choose to share, the names they give them, comments, likes, and reports of comments.

Friends doesn’t ask for last names, addresses, schools, birthdays, or location. Photos should show the art only. Please don’t share photos that show a child’s face.

How it’s used and who sees it

Information is used only to run Friends. Shared art, names, comments, and likes are visible only to your family and the families you connect with. Reported comments are visible to the parent of the artist and to the app owner. The app owner sees parent names and permission forms in order to approve families.

There are no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. Information isn’t sold, and it isn’t given to anyone else. The information is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database for the app owner. The app’s files are hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long it’s kept

Shared art, comments, and likes are kept until a parent deletes them or deletes their family. Reports are kept until a parent or the app owner reviews them. The permission form is kept while your family uses Friends and deleted when you leave.

Your rights as a parent

In the app’s grown-up area you can see what your child has shared, delete any post or comment, stop sharing with any family, and delete your family and all of its information at any time. You can also contact the app owner to ask what is stored or to have it deleted. If you leave, no more information is collected from your child.

Keeping it safe

Pictures are stored privately and can only be opened by approved families through short-lived links. Server rules decide who can see each post, so the app can’t show anything to someone who isn’t connected to your family. A grown-up PIN protects the grown-up area on each device.
